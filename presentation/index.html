Computer Vision Pipeline

4-stage mathematical and colorimetric image processing pipeline in OpenCV.

Stage 1

Gray-World White Balance Color Constancy

Normalizes ambient room lighting (warm tungsten, cold LED) to standard D65 illumination so skin tone measurements are mathematically repeatable across different environments.

Stage 2

YCrCb + HSV Multi-Space Skin Masking

Isolates living human facial tissue from clothing, background, and hair by combining chromaticity thresholds in YCrCb with hue/saturation bounds in HSV color space.

Stage 3

Precise Facial ROI Extraction

Extracts Forehead & T-Zone (sebum detection), Inner Cheeks (redness and moisture barrier analysis), and Malar regions (texture variance and hyperpigmentation spotting).

Stage 4

Biomarker Mathematical Scoring

Computes 6 clinical indices: Specular Glare (Oiliness), Laplacian Variance (Texture), Erythema Delta (Redness), Luminance Gradient (Dryness), Melanin Clusters (Pigmentation).
